Sample 3-Day Itinerary

This is a sample only. Times and activities vary by province and transport availability.

Sample Palawan itinerary
Day Time Activity Destination Notes
1 Morning City tour and breakfast Puerto Princesa, Palawan Visit the city and try local food.
1 Afternoon Underground River tour Puerto Princesa, Palawan Paddle-boat cave tour (booking needed in advance).
2 Morning Travel to El Nido El Nido, Palawan Long road trip; time depends on transport.
2 Afternoon Island hopping El Nido, Palawan Snorkeling and kayaking in the lagoons.
3 Morning Local market and souvenirs El Nido or Puerto Princesa Buy cashew products and handicrafts.
3 Afternoon Departure Puerto Princesa, Palawan Leave extra time before your flight.

Travel Tips

  • Check weather and transport schedules before you go.
  • Bring cash; some areas have limited digital payments.
  • Book tours and stays early.
  • Respect local customs and Indigenous communities.
  • Do not litter on beaches and in parks.
