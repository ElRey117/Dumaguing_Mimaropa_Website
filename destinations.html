Popular Destinations

Four Islands, One Paradise: Where Pristine Beaches, Rich Heritage, and Warm Hearts Welcome You Home

Sample spots from Palawan, Oriental and Occidental Mindoro, Marinduque and Romblon.

El Nido

El Nido, Palawan

Known for towering limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons and clear water in Bacuit Bay. Island-hopping tours are the most popular way to see it.

Plan a Visit

Puerto Princesa Underground River

Puerto Princesa City, Palawan

Part of the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Visitors tour the cave river by paddle boat.

Plan a Visit

Coron

Coron, Palawan

Famous for Kayangan Lake and its clear waters. It is also a top diving area because of its World War II shipwrecks.

Plan a Visit

Puerto Galera

Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro

A popular beach town with coral reefs for snorkeling and diving. It is recognized by UNESCO as a Man and Biosphere Reserve.

Plan a Visit

Apo Reef Natural Park

Sablayan, Occidental Mindoro

One of the largest coral reef systems in the Philippines and a protected natural park. It is known for diving, snorkeling and marine life.

Plan a Visit

Boac Cathedral

Boac, Marinduque

A historic Spanish-era church in the provincial capital. Boac is also one of the towns that hosts the Moriones Festival during Holy Week.

Plan a Visit

Bonbon Beach

Romblon, Romblon

A long sandy beach on Romblon Island with calm, clear water. Romblon province is also famous for its marble.
