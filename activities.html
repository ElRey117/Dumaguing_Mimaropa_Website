Adventure

Explore turquoise lagoons, vibrant coral reefs and scenic trails. Whether you paddle, swim or hike, the islands reward every kind of explorer.

  • Island hopping in El Nido and Coron, Palawan
  • Snorkeling and diving at Apo Reef and Puerto Galera
  • Kayaking in the lagoons of Palawan
  • Hiking scenic trails

Nature

Slow down and enjoy MIMAROPA’s protected forests, quiet waterfalls and long white-sand beaches. Remember to leave every place as clean as you found it.

  • Paddle-boat tour of the Puerto Princesa Underground River
  • Visiting waterfalls and protected forests
  • Relaxing on white-sand beaches such as Bonbon Beach

Culture

Spanish-era churches, colorful festivals and Indigenous traditions tell the story of the region. Join local celebrations with respect and curiosity.

  • Visiting heritage churches such as Boac Cathedral
  • Watching the Moriones Festival in Marinduque during Holy Week
  • Seeing Romblon marble carving
  • Learning about Mangyan and Palawan Indigenous heritage with respect

Food

Taste the islands through fresh seafood, sweet treats and local specialties. Markets and seaside eateries are the best places to try them.

  • Tasting uraro cookies from Marinduque
  • Trying cashew products from Palawan
  • Visiting local markets and seaside eateries
